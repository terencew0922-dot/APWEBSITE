About Knox Grammar School

A century of forging fine young men of character, scholarship and service on Sydney's Upper North Shore.

Our ethos

Virile Agitur — play the man.

Founded in 1924 with proud Scottish Presbyterian roots, Knox Grammar School educates young men from Early Learning to Year 12. Our crest — the saltire of Saint Andrew and the Scottish thistle — carries our motto Virile Agitur: to act with courage, integrity and strength of character.

This Learning Hub extends that tradition beyond the classroom — somewhere every Knoxonian can ask freely, reach trusted resources, and stay connected to the life of the School.
